# Garage Sale & Clearance Kit

# 1. Sale planning checklist

- [ ] Choose date, rain plan, and public posting deadline
- [ ] Decide pickup, delivery, payment, and safety rules
- [ ] Register or notify the relevant local authority if required
- [ ] Sort items into Keep, Donate, Sell, and Discard
- [ ] Photograph items that need individual listings
- [ ] Label items with a temporary storage zone
- [ ] Prepare cashless payment options and a change box
- [ ] Gather tables, signs, bags, markers, tape, and cleaning supplies
- [ ] Advertise the sale and verify that event details are accurate
- [ ] Arrange help for move-out and closeout donation

**Suggested process:**

1. Start with a fair price based on condition and local comparisons.
2. Reduce by roughly 20–30% when buyers lose interest or after a set period.
3. Move unsold items to the donation or removal pile rather than carrying clutter forward.
